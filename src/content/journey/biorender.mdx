I joined BioRender as one of the earliest engineering hires back in 2018.

My "application" was a bit unconventional: I sent my resume along with a security report highlighting vulnerabilities I found, and promised to fix them once onboarded. True to my word, those were my first tickets.

Being part of a YC startup in its infancy meant wearing every hat possible. No two days were the same; I fluctuated between shipping core features, optimizing our CI/CD pipelines, and speaking directly with researchers to bridge the gap between complex science and intuitive UX.

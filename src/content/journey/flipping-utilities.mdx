When the OSRS Wiki announced a real-time pricing API, I saw an opportunity to build the tools I personally wanted as a player.

What began as a simple spreadsheet for tracking item margins has evolved into a comprehensive ecosystem—including a high-traffic web platform and a Discord bot used by thousands of communities. Scaling to 100,000+ users forced me to master high-concurrency systems on a budget, deep process optimization, and the nuances of community-led growth.

While I’ve hung up my own cape in-game, I remain dedicated to the product. My focus now is on pushing the boundaries of market visualization and providing the player base with professional-grade financial insights.

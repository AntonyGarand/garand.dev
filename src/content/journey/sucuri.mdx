Sucuri was my entry point into the tech industry, and I joined during a period of explosive growth for the company. 

I navigated the challenge of finishing my final years of study while simultaneously hunting for critical vulnerabilities. My work focused on identifying and mitigating high-impact vectors like Cross-Site Scripting (XSS), SQL Injection (SQLi), and Remote Code Execution (RCE) across the web ecosystem.

This role also marked the beginning of my journey into technical thought leadership; I began writing my first technical articles and security advisories, translating complex vulnerability research into actionable insights for the broader security community.

outcomeSummary: "An endless feed of puzzles so you can doomscroll something more productive. Ad-free, open source, local-first, and with 53 procedurally generated puzzles for an endless feed of fun!"
overview: "Puzzle Express is a feed of short puzzles you scroll through like a social feed: Sudoku, Nonograms, Bridges, FreeCell, mate-in-two chess, word games and more. Every puzzle is generated from a seed, so there's always a next one, and the daily set is the same for everyone."
problem: "Puzzle apps tend to do one game, push ads, or require an account. I wanted one place with a wide variety of quick puzzles that adapts to your level, keeps your progress without a backend, and runs anywhere."
constraints:
  - "Static site only: no server, no accounts; profile, stats and unfinished games live in localStorage"
  - "Every game generated on the fly from a seed, fast enough to keep the feed instant"
  - "Fully playable offline and installable on mobile"
  - "Bilingual (English and Québec French), including the word games' dictionaries"
approach: "A plain TypeScript app built with Vite, one file per game, auto-registered into the feed. Shared engines (sudokukit, logickit, pathkit, movekit, cardkit, chesskit, wordkit) carry most of the logic, so a new game is mostly its rules and its generator. Each game has five difficulty levels and the feed auto-levels based on how you do."
keyDecisions:
  - decision: "Seeded generation instead of puzzle packs"
    reasoning: "A seed is a whole puzzle: it gives an infinite feed, a shared daily puzzle and shareable links without storing or serving any puzzle data."
    alternatives:
      - "Curated puzzle packs"
      - "A backend serving puzzles"
  - decision: "No UI framework, shared game kits instead"
    reasoning: "The games are mostly grid rendering with their own input handling. Small shared kits per family of games gave more reuse than a component framework would, and kept the bundle small for offline use."

learnings:

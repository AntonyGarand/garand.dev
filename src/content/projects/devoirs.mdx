outcomeSummary: "A learning hub for my kids to learn, backed by the official school curriculum: A safe and fun way to evolve education!"
overview: "Devoirs is a French-language homework companion aligned with Québec's Programme de formation de l'école québécoise (PFEQ). It covers math, French, English, social studies, science and music, with short practice sets, interactive lessons and a daily review."
problem: "Homework apps are not as customized to my child's current level and needs, especially as I'm in the French-speaking province of Quebec. While we do have great resources, tech tends to lag behind: this is my vibe-coded attempt at parsing the education system and enhancing the experience to bridge learning, technology and autonomy!"
constraints:
  - "Aligned skill by skill with the PFEQ and its Progressions des apprentissages"
  - "Usable by a 7-year-old: recorded voice, visual manipulatives, no reading-heavy instructions"
  - "No accounts or backend: progress stays in the browser, with export and import"
  - "Works offline on a tablet once loaded"
approach: "A dependency-free static site written in TypeScript with a small in-house framework. Each skill has practice sets of 8 questions with something to manipulate, a hint, a worked demonstration and an explanation. A daily review interleaves questions and prioritizes what's least mastered or seen longest ago. A parents section maps every skill to the curriculum."
keyDecisions:
  - decision: "No UI framework and no ES modules"
    reasoning: "Classic scripts loaded in order keep the site working even when opened straight from a file and free of runtime dependencies, while TypeScript types still cover the whole code base."
    alternatives:
      - "React or another SPA framework"
  - decision: "Pre-recorded voice with a fallback to the device's voice"
    reasoning: "Device text-to-speech varies a lot between tablets and is often poor in Québec French. Recorded audio, cached by the service worker, gives a consistent voice offline."
techStack:
  - "TypeScript (compiled with tsrs)"
  - "Service worker / PWA"
  - "Web Audio"
  - "oxlint & oxfmt"
  - "GitHub Pages"

learnings:
  - "Designing for a young child means every instruction must be visual or spoken; text is a last resort."
  - "Spaced, interleaved review does more than long blocks of one skill."
